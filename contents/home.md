I am a **master’s student in Communication Engineering at Nanjing University**, where I also received my bachelor’s degree in 2026.

I am a member of the [CITE Robotics Lab](https://shenqiu.njucite.cn/) at Nanjing University, advised by **Associate Professor [Qiu Shen](https://shenqiu.njucite.cn/)** and **Professor [Xun Cao](https://cite.nju.edu.cn/People/Faculty/20190621/i5054.html)**.

My research explores how intelligent systems perceive, understand, and generate motion in the physical world. I am interested in connecting multimodal human perception and 3D motion modeling with robot execution and interaction, bringing together model research and practical systems for more natural human–agent interaction.
